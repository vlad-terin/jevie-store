# PERT Estimate

A three-point estimate as a card while you type: optimistic, likely and pessimistic give the expected time and a range you can promise.

## Type

- “pert 2 4 10”
- “pert 3 5 12 days”
- “three point estimate 1 2 6 weeks”

Expected time is (optimistic + 4 × likely + pessimistic) ÷ 6, the spread (pessimistic − optimistic) ÷ 6, and the range is about 95% (two spreads either side, kept between your optimistic and pessimistic). Numbers in the order optimistic, likely, pessimistic. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
