# Simple Interest

Interest for a number of days, as a card while you type - on an invoice, a late payment or a short loan - by the day count you choose.

## Type

- “interest 5000 at 8% for 45 days”
- “late fee 2500 at 1.5% a month for 60 days”
- “interest 1m at 5.3% for 30 days 360”

Simple interest, not compounded, on actual days over a 365-day year; add “360” for actual/360. A monthly or daily rate is turned into a yearly one. ↵ copies the interest.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
