# Days Supply

How many days a prescription lasts, as a card while you type - from the quantity and the dose - and when it runs out.

## Type

- “days supply 90 tablets 2 a day”
- “days supply 60 tablets 1 tablet twice daily”
- “days supply 30 ml 2.5 ml 3 times a day”

Quantity ÷ (units a dose × doses a day): doses can be “2 a day”, “twice daily”, “3 times a day”, or bid, tid, qid. It gives the day it runs out if started today. Not medical advice. ↵ copies the days.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
