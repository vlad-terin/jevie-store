# Home Projects

How much paint, flooring or concrete a job takes, as cards while you type - with waste and coats counted.

## Type

- “paint 12x14 room 8ft walls”
- “flooring 12x14”
- “tile 10x8 15% waste”
- “concrete 10x10 4 inches”

Paint: the walls of a room less a door (21 sq ft) and two windows (15 each), 350 sq ft a gallon, two coats unless named. Flooring: the floor plus 10% waste unless named, in 20 sq ft boxes. Concrete: cubic yards, and 80 lb bags at 0.6 cu ft each. Feet throughout. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
