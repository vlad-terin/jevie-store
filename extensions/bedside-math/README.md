# Bedside Math

Ward numbers as cards while you type: corrected QT (Bazett, Fridericia), anion gap, mean arterial pressure, corrected calcium and maintenance fluids by the 4-2-1 rule.

## Type

- “qtc 400 hr 75”
- “anion gap na 140 cl 104 hco3 24 albumin 2.5”
- “map 120/80”
- “corrected calcium 8.0 albumin 2.5”
- “maintenance fluids 25 kg”

QTc by Bazett (QT ÷ √RR), Fridericia (QT ÷ ∛RR) and Framingham, from a heart rate or an RR interval. Anion gap Na − (Cl + HCO3), albumin-corrected when given. Mean arterial pressure DBP + (SBP − DBP) ÷ 3. Corrected calcium by Payne's formula (mg/dL, g/dL). Maintenance fluids by Holliday-Segar (4-2-1 mL/h and 100-50-20 mL a day). For checking arithmetic, not diagnosis: your lab and protocols decide. ↵ copies the result.
