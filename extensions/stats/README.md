# Stats

The numbers of a list at a glance, as a card while you type: count, mean, median, standard deviation, quartiles, min and max - typed, or from the clipboard.

## Type

- “stats 3 5 7 9 11 40”
- “stats 1.2, 3.4, 5.6”
- “stats clipboard”

The standard deviation is the sample one (n − 1). Quartiles use the linear method spreadsheets use. Outliers are values past 1.5 IQR. “stats clipboard” reads a pasted column or row. ↵ copies the summary.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac (the clipboard only when you say “clipboard”). It changes nothing.
