# Solar Size

How big a solar system covers your electricity, as a card while you type - from kWh a month and your sun hours - in kW and panels.

## Type

- “solar 900 kwh a month”
- “solar 12000 kwh a year 5 sun hours 430w panels”

Daily use (from a month, a year or a day) ÷ peak sun hours (4.5 unless you name yours; the US runs about 3.5 to 6) ÷ 0.8 for losses gives the system in kW; panels at 400 W unless named, about 21.5 sq ft each. Shade, tilt and your utility's rules are a site survey's to decide. ↵ copies the size.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
