# Court Deadline

A deadline counted as courts count it, as a card while you type: N days from a trigger date, moved to the next business day when it lands on a weekend or US federal holiday.

## Type

- “deadline 21 days from oct 1”
- “court deadline 30 days from 2026-12-01”
- “deadline 14 days after nov 20”

It counts as FRCP 6(a)(1) does: the trigger day is left out, every day counts, and a last day on a Saturday, Sunday or US federal holiday (observed) runs to the next day that is none of them. It names what it moved past. State and local rules, extra days for service by mail, and court closures differ: check the rule that applies. Not legal advice. ↵ copies the date.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
