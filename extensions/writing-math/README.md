# Writing Math

A writer's sums as cards while you type: how many pages a word count makes, and what a translation or a piece costs at a rate per word.

## Type

- “pages 2500 words”
- “pages 80000 words manuscript”
- “translation 2500 words at 0.12”

Pages assume 250 words double-spaced, 500 single-spaced and about 300 in a trade paperback. The fee is words × rate, with how many days it takes at 2,500 words a day. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
