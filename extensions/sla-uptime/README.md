# SLA Uptime

What an uptime promise allows in downtime, as a card while you type - per year, month, week and day - and what an outage did to this month's uptime.

## Type

- “99.9% uptime”
- “sla 99.95%”
- “four nines”
- “downtime 43 minutes this month”

A year is 365.25 days, a month a twelfth of it. The downtime card shows the uptime an outage leaves and the highest common SLA (99%, 99.5%, 99.9%, 99.95%, 99.99%, 99.999%) still met. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
