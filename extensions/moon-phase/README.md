# Moon Phase

Tonight's moon as a card while you type: its phase, how full it is, and the next full and new moon - worked out on your Mac.

## Type

- “moon phase”
- “next full moon”
- “moon on 2026-12-25”

↵ copies the line: `Waxing gibbous, 81% lit · full Fri, Oct 16 · new Fri, Oct 30`. Worked out from the length of the moon's month, so it is right to within about a day.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
