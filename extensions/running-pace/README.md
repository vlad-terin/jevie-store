# Running Pace

A runner's pace, finish time or distance as a card while you type - per kilometre and per mile - for 5k, 10k, half and full marathons or any distance.

## Type

- “pace 5k in 25 min”
- “pace 10 miles in 1:25:00”
- “half marathon in 1:45”
- “marathon at 5:30 per km”

A distance (5k, 10k, half, marathon, or km and miles) and a time gives the pace a km and a mile; a distance and a pace (“at 5:30 per km”) gives the finish time. It also shows what that pace makes of the usual race distances: an even pace, not a race prediction. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
