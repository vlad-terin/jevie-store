# Product Metrics

A product manager's numbers as cards while you type: RICE scores, NPS, churn and retention, and a sprint's capacity.

## Type

- “rice reach 2000 impact 2 confidence 80% effort 3”
- “nps 120 promoters 40 passives 30 detractors”
- “churn 40 of 1200”
- “capacity 5 people 10 days 70% 3 days off”

RICE is reach × impact × confidence ÷ effort. NPS is % promoters minus % detractors. Churn gives retention and the yearly loss it implies. Capacity is people × days less days off, times a focus factor (70% unless named). ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
