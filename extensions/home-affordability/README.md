# Home Affordability

How much house an income can carry, as a card while you type - by the 28/36 rule, with the payment, the down payment and taxes counted.

## Type

- “afford 120k income 6.5% 20% down”
- “afford 150k income 7% 10% down debts 600”
- “how much house can i afford 150k”

By the 28/36 rule: housing at most 28% of gross monthly income, all debts 36%. Property tax and insurance count as 1.5% of the price a year unless you name a rate (“tax 2%”). The rate is 6.5%, 20% down and 30 years unless you say otherwise. A rule of thumb, not a lender's answer. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
