# Z-Score

A value's z-score and percentile as a card while you type, from the mean and standard deviation - or the value at a percentile.

## Type

- “z score 85 mean 70 sd 10”
- “percentile 90 mean 100 sd 15”
- “95th percentile mean 70 sd 10”

A z-score is (value − mean) ÷ standard deviation; the percentile is the share of a normal distribution below it. The percentile card goes the other way: the value at a percentile. It assumes a normal distribution. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
