# Inventory Math

When to reorder and how much, as cards while you type: the reorder point from daily use, lead time and safety stock, and the economic order quantity.

## Type

- “reorder point 20 a day lead 7 days safety 50”
- “eoq demand 1200 order cost 50 holding 2”

The reorder point is daily use × lead time in days + safety stock. The economic order quantity is √(2 × yearly demand × cost of one order ÷ cost to hold a unit for a year), with orders a year and the yearly ordering and holding cost. ↵ copies the number.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
