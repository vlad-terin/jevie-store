# Construction Math

Roof pitch and rafter length, and a staircase's risers and treads by the US residential code, as cards while you type.

## Type

- “roof pitch 6/12 run 12 ft”
- “pitch 8/12”
- “stairs 108 inches rise”
- “stairs 9 ft rise”

Pitch gives the roof angle and, with a run, the rafter length before overhang (and the rise). Stairs divide the total rise into the fewest risers of at most 7¾″, with 10″ treads, as the US residential code (IRC R311.7) allows. Check your local code. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
