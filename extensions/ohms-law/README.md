# Ohm's Law

Volts, amps, ohms and watts from any two, as a card while you type - and the resistor an LED needs.

## Type

- “ohms 12v 2a”
- “ohms law 12v 6 ohms”
- “ohms 2a 60w”
- “led resistor 5v 2v 20ma”

Any two of volts, amps, ohms and watts give the other two (V = I × R, P = V × I). The LED card gives the resistor for a supply, an LED's forward voltage and its current, rounded up to the next E12 standard value, with the power it burns. ↵ copies the answer.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
