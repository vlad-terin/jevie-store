# AC Size

How big an air conditioner a room needs, in BTU, as a card while you type - by ENERGY STAR's room chart, adjusted for sun, people and a kitchen.

## Type

- “btu 400 sq ft”
- “ac size 300 sq ft sunny kitchen 4 people”
- “btu 12x15 room”
- “ac size 35 m2”

It uses ENERGY STAR's room air conditioner chart (100 to 2,500 sq ft), then shady −10%, sunny +10%, 600 BTU for each person over two, and 4,000 for a kitchen. A room can be given as an area in sq ft or m², or as feet by feet. ↵ copies the BTU.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
