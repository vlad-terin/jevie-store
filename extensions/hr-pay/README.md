# Overtime & PTO

Overtime pay and paid-time-off accrual, as cards while you type - time-and-a-half past 40 hours, and how much leave has built up.

## Type

- “overtime 32/h 46 hours”
- “overtime $28 an hour 52 hours double after 50”
- “pto 15 days a year 3 months”
- “pto 120 hours a year biweekly”

Overtime is time and a half past 40 hours a week, the US federal rule; name a double-time line (“double after 50”) if yours has one. Some states also count overtime by the day, which this does not. PTO accrues evenly through the year; carry-over and caps are your employer's. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
