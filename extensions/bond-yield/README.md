# Bond Yield

A bond's current yield and yield to maturity from its price, as a card while you type - and the price from a yield.

## Type

- “bond price 950 face 1000 coupon 5% 10 years”
- “bond yield 6% face 1000 coupon 5% 10 years”

Coupons are paid twice a year, as US bonds pay. A price gives the yield to maturity and the current yield; a yield gives the price. Face is 1000 unless you name it. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
