# Hacker News

Hacker News front page, new, best, Ask and Show - a port of Raycast's Hacker News extension.

## Say

- “hacker news”
- “hn best”
- “hn show”

## Settings

- **Stories**: How many stories a list shows, up to 100.
- **Opens on**

“settings for hacker-news” changes them; secrets are kept in the Keychain.
