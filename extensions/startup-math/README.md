# Startup Math

A founder's numbers as cards while you type: months of runway from cash and burn, and what a priced round does to ownership.

## Type

- “runway 400k burn 35k”
- “runway 1.2m burn 90k revenue 20k growing 5%”
- “raise 2m at 8m pre”
- “raise 2m at 8m pre 10% pool own 60%”

Runway is cash over net burn, month by month, with revenue growing if a rate is named; it says “default alive” when revenue catches up first. A priced round gives the post-money, the investors' share, and with “own 60%” a holder's stake after. A new option pool (“10% pool”) comes out of the existing holders' share. SAFEs and notes are not modelled. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
