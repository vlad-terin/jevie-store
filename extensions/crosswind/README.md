# Crosswind

A runway's crosswind and headwind components from the wind, as a card while you type - with gusts - for pilots.

## Type

- “crosswind wind 270 at 15 runway 24”
- “crosswind 310/18g25 runway 27”
- “xwind 090 12 rwy 36”

Crosswind is the wind speed × sin of the angle between the wind and the runway (its number × 10), headwind × cos; a negative headwind is a tailwind. Gusts give the worst case too. Use the same reference for both (magnetic, as ATIS and runways are). Check it against your aircraft's demonstrated crosswind. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
