# Percent Change

The percentage change between two numbers as a card while you type - increase or decrease, and the difference - plus percentage points between two rates.

## Type

- “percent change 120 to 150”
- “% change 80 to 60”
- “change 4.5% to 5.25%”
- “increase from 1.2m to 1.5m”

The change is (new − old) ÷ |old|, with the plain difference beside it. Between two percentages it also gives the change in percentage points: 4.5% to 5.25% is +0.75 points but +16.67% relative. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
