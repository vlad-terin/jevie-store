# Readability

How easy a text is to read, as a card while you type: Flesch reading ease, the school grade, the longest sentence and the reading time.

## Type

- “readability The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.”
- “readability clipboard”

60 to 70 is plain English, which most readers manage; under 30 reads like a legal or academic text. The longest sentence is shown when it runs past 25 words. ↵ copies the score.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac (the clipboard is read only when you type “readability clipboard”). It changes nothing.
