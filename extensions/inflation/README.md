# Inflation

What money from one year is worth in another, as a card while you type - from the US consumer price index (CPI-U, FRED).

## Type

- “inflation $100 in 1990”
- “$50 in 1975 dollars”
- “inflation 1000 from 2000 to 2020”

It uses US CPI-U annual averages from FRED (1913 to now; the current year is the average of its months so far). ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

fred.stlouisfed.org (the CPI series, once a month). It changes nothing.
