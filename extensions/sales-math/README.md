# Sales Math

Commission and quota attainment as cards while you type - flat rates, accelerators past quota, and how much is left to hit the number.

## Type

- “commission 85000 at 8%”
- “commission 620k at 10% quota 500k accelerator 1.5x”
- “quota 420k of 500k”
- “quota 420k of 500k 23 days left”

Commission is flat, or with a quota and an accelerator, the rate is multiplied for sales past quota. Quota shows attainment, the gap, and with days left (weeks count as five working days) the pace a day needs. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
