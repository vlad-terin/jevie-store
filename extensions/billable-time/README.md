# Billable Time

Time in billable tenths of an hour as a card while you type - 47 minutes is 0.8 - and what it bills at a rate.

## Type

- “billable 47 min”
- “billable 1:20 at 450”
- “bill 6.3 hours at 450”
- “billable 50 min quarters”

Time is recorded in tenths of an hour (6-minute units) unless you say quarters, any part of a unit billed as a whole one: 47 minutes is 0.8. With a rate it gives the amount. Your engagement letter decides the increment. ↵ copies the hours (and amount).

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
