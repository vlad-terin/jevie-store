# Late Fee

The late fee on an overdue invoice as a card while you type - a monthly percentage for the days late, or a flat fee - and the new total.

## Type

- “late fee 2000 1.5% monthly 45 days”
- “late fee 3500 18% a year 60 days”
- “late fee 1200 flat 50 plus 1% monthly 30 days”

Simple interest for the days late: a monthly rate over 30-day months, a yearly one over 365 days, plus a flat fee if given. A late fee has to be in your terms, and many states and countries cap it. ↵ copies the new total.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
