# Photo Math

A photographer's sums as cards while you type: depth of field and hyperfocal distance for a lens, and the shutter speed that keeps an exposure when the aperture or ISO changes.

## Type

- “dof 50mm f/1.8 3m”
- “depth of field 85mm f2 2m aps-c”
- “exposure f/8 1/125 iso 100 to f/4”
- “exposure f/8 1/125 iso 100 to iso 400”

Depth of field uses the thin-lens formulas: hyperfocal = f² ÷ (N × c) + f, with a circle of confusion of 0.03 mm for full frame unless you say APS-C (0.02) or Micro Four Thirds (0.015); it gives the near and far limits of sharpness. Exposure keeps the same light: the new shutter is the old × (new f-number ÷ old)² × (old ISO ÷ new), rounded to the nearest full stop too, with the EV at ISO 100. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
