# Training Zones

Heart-rate zones from your age (and resting rate), and cycling power zones and watts per kilo from your FTP - as cards while you type.

## Type

- “heart rate zones age 35 resting 60”
- “hr zones 42”
- “ftp zones 250”
- “watts per kg 250w 75kg”

Maximum heart rate is estimated by Tanaka (208 − 0.7 × age) unless you give one (“max 188”). With a resting rate the zones are Karvonen (a share of heart-rate reserve), otherwise a share of max. Power zones are Coggan's seven, from FTP. Watts per kilo is power over weight. Estimates, not medical advice. ↵ copies the zones.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
