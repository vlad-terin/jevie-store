# Garden Math

How much mulch, soil or compost a bed takes, and how many plants fit at a spacing, as cards while you type - in cubic yards, cubic feet and bags.

## Type

- “mulch 10x12 ft 3 inches”
- “soil 4x8 ft 10 inches”
- “plants 4x8 ft 12 inch spacing”

A bed's width and length in feet and a depth in inches gives cubic feet, cubic yards and 2 cu ft bags (add 10–20% for soil that will settle). Plants are counted on a square grid at the spacing; offset rows fit about 15% more. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
