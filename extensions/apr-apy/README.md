# APR & APY

The APY an APR earns, or the APR behind an APY, as a card while you type - compounded monthly, daily or as you say.

## Type

- “apr to apy 5%”
- “apr 5% daily”
- “apy 5.12% to apr”
- “apy to apr 4.5% quarterly”

APY = (1 + APR ÷ n)^n − 1, where n is how often it compounds a year: monthly unless you say daily, weekly, quarterly, semiannually, yearly or continuously. An APY is turned back into the APR behind it. ↵ copies the converted rate.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
