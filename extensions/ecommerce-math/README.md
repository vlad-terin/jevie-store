# E-commerce Math

An online store's unit economics as cards while you type: profit per order after costs, shipping and fees, and the break-even ROAS your margin allows.

## Type

- “profit per order price 40 cost 12 shipping 6 fees 3%”
- “order profit price 40 cost 12 fees 2.9% + 0.30 ads 8”
- “break even roas margin 40%”
- “break even roas price 50 cost 20”

Profit per order is the price less the product, shipping, payment fees (a percentage, plus a flat fee if given), ads and other costs. Break-even ROAS is 1 ÷ margin: below it, each sale from ads loses money, before repeat purchases. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
