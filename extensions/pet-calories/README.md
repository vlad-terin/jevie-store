# Pet Calories

How many calories a dog or cat needs a day, as a card while you type - resting energy × a life-stage factor - for neutered, intact, puppies, kittens and weight loss.

## Type

- “dog calories 20 kg neutered”
- “dog calories 45 lb active”
- “puppy calories 8 kg”
- “cat calories 10 lb weight loss”

Resting energy is 70 × weight in kg^0.75, times the usual factor: for dogs neutered 1.6, intact 1.8, weight loss 1.0, active 3.0, senior 1.4, growing 2.5; for cats neutered 1.2, intact 1.4, weight loss 0.8. A starting point to adjust by body condition, with your vet. ↵ copies the calories.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
