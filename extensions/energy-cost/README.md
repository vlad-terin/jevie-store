# Energy Cost

What running an appliance costs and how long an EV takes to charge, as cards while you type - kWh, cost a day, a month and a year.

## Type

- “energy 1500w 4 hours at 0.15”
- “energy 60 w 8 h a day at 0.30”
- “ev charge 75 kwh at 11 kw”
- “ev charge 60 kwh 20% to 80% at 7.4 kw at 0.12”

Appliance cost is watts × hours a day, at a price per kWh, for a day, a 30.4-day month and a year. EV charging counts about 10% charging losses; DC fast chargers also slow down past about 80%, which this does not model. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
