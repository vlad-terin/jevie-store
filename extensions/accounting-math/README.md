# Accounting Math

VAT and sales tax in and out of a price, and depreciation schedules - straight-line and double-declining - as cards while you type.

## Type

- “vat 20% on 100”
- “sales tax 8.25% on 59.99”
- “remove vat 20% from 120”
- “depreciation 50000 over 5 years”
- “depreciation 50000 over 5 years salvage 5000 double declining”

Adding tax gives net, tax and gross. Removing it finds the tax already inside a gross price: gross ÷ (1 + rate), not gross minus the rate. Depreciation is straight-line unless “double declining”; that schedule's last year takes the book value down to salvage. ↵ copies the gross, the net or the schedule.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
