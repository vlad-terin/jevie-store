# Medical Bill

What you pay of a medical bill after insurance, as a card while you type - deductible, coinsurance, copay and out-of-pocket maximum.

## Type

- “medical bill 5000 deductible 1500 coinsurance 20%”
- “medical bill 12000 deductible 2000 coinsurance 30% oop max 6000”

The usual US order: the deductible (what is left of it this year) first, then coinsurance on the rest, plus a copay, all capped at the out-of-pocket maximum. It assumes the bill is the allowed amount in network; your plan's explanation of benefits is the real answer. ↵ copies what you pay.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
