# Retirement

Your financial-independence number and what savings can pay out, as cards while you type - the 4% rule, and how long a portfolio lasts.

## Type

- “fire 60k expenses”
- “fire 60k expenses 200k saved saving 3k a month at 7%”
- “retire with 1.5m at 4%”

The FIRE number is 25 times yearly spending (the 4% rule). With savings and a monthly amount, it also says how many years until you reach it (7% a year unless you name a rate). The withdrawal card says what a pot pays at a rate. Before tax and inflation. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
