# Clinical Calculators

BMI with body surface area, a due date from the last period, and creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault), as cards while you type - worked out on your Mac.

## Type

- “bmi 180 cm 75 kg”
- “bmi 5'11 170 lb”
- “due date lmp 2026-03-01”
- “edd lmp mar 1”
- “crcl 65 years 70 kg creatinine 1.2 male”
- “dose 15 mg/kg 20 kg in 3 doses max 500 mg”
- “drip 1000 ml over 8 hours 20 gtt”
- “glucose 100 mg/dl”, “glucose 7.2 mmol/l”
- “a1c 7”

Each card names its formula: BMI with WHO bands and body surface area by Mosteller, the due date by Naegele's rule (last period + 280 days) with the weeks along today, creatinine clearance by Cockcroft-Gault on actual body weight, a dose per kg (split into doses and capped at a named maximum), an infusion's mL/h and drops a minute, glucose between mg/dL and mmol/L, and A1c to estimated average glucose (ADAG). A creatinine above 30 is read as µmol/L. These are calculators, not medical advice. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
