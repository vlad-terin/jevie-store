# Pet Age

A dog's or a cat's age in human years, as a card while you type - by the vets' rule of thumb, and for dogs the 2019 epigenetic-clock formula too.

## Type

- “dog age 5”
- “large dog age 8”
- “cat age 12”

Dogs: 15 human years at one, 24 at two, then about 5 a year (4 for small breeds, 6 for large, 7 for giant), the vets' rule of thumb. Also 16 × ln(age) + 31, the epigenetic-clock formula from Wang et al. (2019, Labradors). Cats: 15, 24, then 4 a year. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
