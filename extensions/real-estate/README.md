# Real Estate Returns

A property's numbers as cards while you type: cap rate, cash-on-cash return, gross rental yield and the 1% rule, and price per square foot.

## Type

- “cap rate noi 24000 price 400000”
- “cash on cash 30000 down 2400 cash flow”
- “rental yield rent 2000 price 350000”
- “450k for 1800 sq ft”, “320000 for 95 m2”

Cap rate is net operating income (rent less running costs, before the mortgage) over price. Cash-on-cash is a year's cash flow after the mortgage over the cash put in. Rental yield is a year's rent over price, with the 1% rule (monthly rent at least 1% of the price). Price per area gives both a square foot and a square metre. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
