# Voltage Drop

A wire run's voltage drop as a card while you type - copper or aluminium, by AWG, one way length, volts and amps - and the gauge that keeps it under 3%.

## Type

- “voltage drop 12v 20a 30 ft 10 awg”
- “voltage drop 120v 15a 100 ft 12 awg”
- “voltage drop 240v 40a 150 ft 6 awg aluminum”

Drop = 2 × one-way length × current × the wire's resistance, from NEC Chapter 9 Table 8 for copper (aluminium about 1.64 times), for DC or single-phase AC. Over 3% it names the smallest gauge that keeps it under. Ampacity, temperature and code are separate checks. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
