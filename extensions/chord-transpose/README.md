# Chord Transpose

Chords moved to a new key as a card while you type - by semitones or to a key - and what a capo makes of the shapes you play.

## Type

- “transpose G C D up 2”
- “transpose Am F C G down 3”
- “transpose G C Em D to A”
- “capo 2 G C Em D”

Chords move by semitones (“up 2”, “down 3”) or to a key (“to A”, from the first chord's root), keeping each chord's quality and bass note. Sharps are used unless the chords or the key use flats. “capo 2 G C Em D” gives what those shapes sound as. ↵ copies the new chords.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
