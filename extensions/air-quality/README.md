# Air Quality

A card under the field as you type: the air quality somewhere right now. It
shows the US AQI with its band ("Good", "Unhealthy for sensitive groups", …),
what the band means for being outside, and the pollutants behind it (PM2.5,
PM10, ozone, NO₂). From [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), no key. A place
is looked up once; a reading is kept an hour.

## Type

- “air quality berlin”
- “aqi delhi”
- “air quality in san francisco”

↵ copies the line: `Berlin: AQI 34, good`.

## Reaches

geocoding-api.open-meteo.com and air-quality-api.open-meteo.com. It changes
nothing.
