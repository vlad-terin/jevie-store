# Car Lease

A car lease's monthly payment as a card while you type - from the price, residual, money factor and term - with the APR the money factor hides.

## Type

- “lease 35000 residual 58% money factor 0.0025 36 months”
- “lease 42000 msrp 45000 residual 60% mf 0.00175 36 months down 2000 tax 8%”

A US lease payment: depreciation ((price − down) − residual) ÷ months, plus a rent charge ((price − down) + residual) × money factor, plus sales tax on the payment if given. The residual is a share of MSRP, or of the price when no MSRP is given. A money factor × 2400 is roughly its APR. Acquisition fees and how your state taxes leases differ. ↵ copies the payment.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
