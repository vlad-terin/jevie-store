# Loan Payoff

What paying extra on a mortgage or loan saves, as a card while you type: the interest saved and how much sooner it is paid off.

## Type

- “mortgage 400k 6.5% 30 years extra 500”
- “loan 25k 7% 5 years extra 100 a month”

It works out the monthly payment, then pays it plus the extra every month until the loan is gone, and compares that with paying the minimum. Jevie's own loan card answers the payment alone (“mortgage 400k 6.5% 30 years”). ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
