# Caffeine

How much caffeine is still in you at bedtime, as a card while you type - from the cups you had and when - at a 5-hour half-life.

## Type

- “caffeine 200mg at 9am bedtime 11pm”
- “caffeine 95mg 8am 95mg 2pm bedtime 10:30pm”
- “caffeine 2 coffees 9am bedtime 11pm”

Each dose halves every 5 hours (a typical half-life; it runs about 2 to 10 hours for different people), added up at bedtime. Doses in mg, or as coffees (95 mg), espressos or shots (63), teas (47), colas (34) or energy drinks (80). Not medical advice. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
