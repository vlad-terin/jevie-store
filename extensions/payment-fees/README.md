# Payment Fees

What you keep after a payment processor's fee, as a card while you type - and what to charge to keep a round amount. Stripe's standard rate, or your own.

## Type

- “stripe fee 100”
- “fee 250 at 3.49% + 0.49”
- “charge to receive 500”

Stripe's standard US online rate, 2.9% + 30¢, unless you type a rate (“at 3.49% + 0.49”); processors change their rates, and many accounts have negotiated ones. It also says what to charge for a round amount to land. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
