# Transfer Time

How long a download, upload or copy takes at a connection speed, as a card while you type - and at a realistic 80%.

## Type

- “download 50 gb at 100 mbps”
- “upload 500 mb at 20 mbps”
- “transfer 2 tb at 1 gbps”
- “copy 40 gib at 250 mb/s”

Size over speed: KB, MB, GB and TB in powers of 1000 (KiB, MiB, GiB, TiB in 1024), speed in bits a second (kbps, Mbps, Gbps) or bytes a second (KB/s, MB/s, GB/s). Real connections rarely run at full speed, so it gives the time at 80% too. ↵ copies the time.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
