# Recipe Scale

A recipe scaled to a new number of servings as a card while you type - double, halve or 4 to 6 - with the amounts in kitchen fractions.

## Type

- “scale recipe 4 to 6: 2 cups flour, 1 tsp salt, 3 eggs”
- “double 1 1/2 cups sugar, 2 eggs”
- “halve 3/4 cup oil, 3 eggs”

Each amount (whole numbers, decimals, fractions like 1 1/2, or ½ ¼ ¾) is multiplied by the new servings ÷ the old, or by double, triple, halve or quarter, and shown to the nearest eighth. Ingredients are split on commas. Baking times and pan sizes do not scale the same way. ↵ copies the list.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
