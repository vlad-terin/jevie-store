# Restaurant Math

A dish's food-cost percentage, and the menu price that hits a target food cost, as cards while you type.

## Type

- “food cost 4.50 price 16”
- “menu price cost 4.50 at 30% food cost”

Food cost is the plate's ingredient cost over its price; most restaurants aim for 28–35%. The menu-price card divides the plate cost by a target food cost and rounds up to the next .49 or .99. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
