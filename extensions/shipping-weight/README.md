# Shipping Weight

A parcel's dimensional weight and the weight carriers will bill, as a card while you type - inches and pounds, or centimetres and kilos.

## Type

- “dim weight 20x16x12 in 18 lb”
- “dim weight 20x16x12 in usps”
- “volumetric 50x40x30 cm 8 kg”

Dimensional weight is length × width × height ÷ a divisor: 139 for UPS and FedEx daily rates in inches and pounds (“usps” uses 166), 5000 in centimetres and kilos. Carriers bill the greater of that and the actual weight, rounded up. Your account's divisor may differ (“divisor 166”). ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
