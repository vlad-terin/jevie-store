# 50/30/20 Budget

A take-home pay split by the 50/30/20 rule, as a card while you type: needs, wants and savings - or your own split.

## Type

- “50/30/20 6200”
- “budget 50/30/20 4800 a month”
- “budget 70/20/10 3000”

Needs 50% (rent, bills, food, minimum debt payments), wants 30%, savings and extra debt payments 20%. Name another split as needs/wants/savings (“70/20/10”); it must add up to 100. ↵ copies the split.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
