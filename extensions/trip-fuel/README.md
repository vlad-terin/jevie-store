# Trip Fuel

What a drive costs in fuel, as a card while you type - miles and mpg or km and L/100 km, at a price - and split between the people in the car.

## Type

- “fuel cost 300 miles at 30 mpg 3.50”
- “gas cost 300 mi 25 mpg 3.89 round trip”
- “trip cost 450 km 7 l/100km 1.80 split 3”

Distance in miles or km, economy in mpg, L/100 km or km/L, and the price a gallon (with mpg) or a litre (otherwise). “round trip” doubles the distance; “split 3” divides the cost. Fuel only. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
