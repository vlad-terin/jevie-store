# Email Subject

How an email subject line will land, as a card while you type: its length on a phone, spam-trigger words, shouting, emoji and the preview.

## Type

- “subject line Last chance: 50% off ends tonight”
- “email subject Your October update”

The score starts at 100 and loses points for running past a phone's ~40 characters or an inbox's 60, for each spam-trigger word (free, act now, limited time, guaranteed, last chance…), for words in capitals, and for several exclamation marks or emoji. It also shows what a phone would display. ↵ copies the verdict.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
