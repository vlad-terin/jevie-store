# Coffee Ratio

How much coffee and water a brew takes, as a card while you type: a ratio, a cup size, or the grams you have.

## Type

- “coffee 1:16 500 ml”
- “coffee 20 g at 1:15”
- “brew ratio 30g 500g”

A ratio of 1:16 unless named. Water in mL, L, oz or cups gives the coffee; coffee in grams gives the water; two weights give the ratio. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
