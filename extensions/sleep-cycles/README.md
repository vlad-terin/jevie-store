# Sleep Cycles

When to go to bed for a wake-up time, or when to wake for a bedtime, in whole 90-minute sleep cycles - as a card while you type.

## Type

- “bedtime for 7am”
- “bedtime for 6:30”
- “sleep cycles 11pm”
- “wake times 23:30”

Sleep runs in cycles of about 90 minutes, and waking at the end of one feels easier. A bedtime “for 7am” gives when to be in bed for 6, 5 or 4 cycles (with 15 minutes to fall asleep); “sleep cycles 11pm” gives when to wake. People vary: a rough guide, not medical advice. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
