# One-Rep Max

Your estimated one-rep max from a set, as a card while you type - Epley and Brzycki - with the weights for training percentages.

## Type

- “1rm 100 kg 5 reps”
- “one rep max 225 lb 8 reps”
- “1rm 140 x 3”

It averages Epley, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), and Brzycki, weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps), and lists 95% to 70% of it for programming. Most reliable at 10 reps or fewer. ↵ copies the max.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
