# Pool Volume

How much water a pool or hot tub holds, as a card while you type - rectangular, round or oval, with shallow and deep ends - in gallons and litres.

## Type

- “pool volume 16x32 ft 3 to 6 ft deep”
- “pool volume round 24 ft 4 ft deep”
- “pool volume oval 15x30 ft 4 ft deep”

Surface area (a rectangle, a circle by its diameter, or an oval as π ÷ 4 of its rectangle) × the average of the shallow and deep ends, in US gallons (7.48 a cubic foot) and litres. ↵ copies the gallons.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
