# Baking

Cups to grams by ingredient, and a dough's hydration and baker's percentages, as cards while you type - from King Arthur Baking's weight chart.

## Type

- “2 cups flour in grams”
- “1/2 cup butter to grams”
- “250 g sugar in cups”
- “hydration 500g flour 350g water”
- “hydration 1000 flour 700 water 20 salt 10 yeast”

Cup weights come from King Arthur Baking's ingredient weight chart (flour 120 g, sugar 198 g, butter 227 g a US cup …). They are approximate, since how an ingredient is scooped changes it. Fractions work (1/2, 1 1/4); so do tablespoons and teaspoons. Hydration is water over flour; every ingredient is also shown as a baker's percentage. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
