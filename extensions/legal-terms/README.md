# Legal Terms

What a legal term or a piece of Latin in a contract or judgment means, in plain English, as a card while you type.

## Type

- “legal term res judicata”
- “legal term force majeure”
- “legal mens rea”

About 65 terms that come up in contracts, filings and judgments: Latin (res judicata, prima facie, inter alia) and plain terms of art (estoppel, indemnify, liquidated damages, without prejudice). The summaries describe common-law usage. They are not legal advice, and the meaning can differ by jurisdiction. ↵ copies the definition.
