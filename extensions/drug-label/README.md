# Drug Label

A medicine's FDA label at a glance as a card while you type: its brand and generic names, what it is for, how it is taken, and whether it carries a boxed warning.

## Type

- “drug ibuprofen”
- “drug lipitor”
- “fda label metformin”

It shows the brand and generic names, whether it is prescription or over the counter, the form and route, the first line of what it is indicated for, and whether the label carries a boxed warning. The most recent label is used. It is reference, not medical advice. ↵ copies the full label's DailyMed link.

## Reaches

api.fda.gov (openFDA). It changes nothing.
