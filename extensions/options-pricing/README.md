# Options Pricing

An option's fair price and Greeks by Black-Scholes, as a card while you type - calls and puts, with delta, gamma, theta, vega and the breakeven.

## Type

- “call 100 strike 105 30 days 25% vol”
- “put 50 strike 45 60 days 40% vol 4% rate”

Black-Scholes for a European option with no dividends. The rate is 4% unless you name one. Theta is a day's decay, vega the change per point of volatility. A model price, not a market quote. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
