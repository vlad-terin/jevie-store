# Ideal Body Weight

Ideal and adjusted body weight for dosing as a card while you type - by Devine, from height and sex, and the adjusted weight when actual weight is well over it.

## Type

- “ibw 180 cm male”
- “ideal body weight 5 ft 4 female 90 kg”
- “ibw 70 in male 120 kg”

Ideal body weight by Devine: 50 kg for men or 45.5 kg for women, plus 2.3 kg for each inch over 5 feet (from cm, feet and inches, or inches). With an actual weight it gives the adjusted body weight, IBW + 0.4 × (actual − IBW), and notes when actual is under 120% of ideal. For dosing arithmetic, not diagnosis. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
