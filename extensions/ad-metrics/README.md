# Ad Metrics

The marketing numbers, as cards while you type: ROAS, CAC, CTR, CPC, CPM, conversion rate, and customer lifetime value.

## Type

- “roas revenue 5000 spend 1200”
- “cac spend 12000 customers 300”
- “ctr 120 clicks 8000 impressions”
- “cpm spend 1200 impressions 400k”
- “conversion 45 of 1200”
- “ltv 50 a month 24 months 70% margin cac 300”

Words may name each number (spend, revenue, clicks, impressions, customers, margin, months), or they are read in the order shown. Amounts take k, m and b. LTV with a CAC also gives the LTV:CAC ratio (3:1 or better is the usual bar). ↵ copies the answer.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
