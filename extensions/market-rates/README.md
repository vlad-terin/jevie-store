# Market Rates

The rates and numbers markets watch, as a card while you type: Treasury yields and the curve, the Fed funds rate, SOFR, 30-year mortgages, inflation and unemployment - from FRED.

## Type

- “10 year treasury”
- “2 year treasury”
- “yield curve”
- “fed funds rate”
- “sofr”
- “mortgage rates”
- “us inflation rate”
- “unemployment rate”
- “rates”

Each card shows the latest value, its change over a week and a year, and the date. The yield curve card flags an inversion (10-year below 2-year). Inflation is CPI over twelve months. All from FRED, no key. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

fred.stlouisfed.org. It changes nothing.
