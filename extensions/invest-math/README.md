# Investing Math

An investor's quick sums as cards while you type: the rule of 72 (and the exact doubling time), dividend yield, and the tax-equivalent yield of a tax-free bond.

## Type

- “rule of 72 8%”
- “dividend yield 2.40 price 80”
- “dividend 0.60 quarterly price 80”
- “tax equivalent yield 3% 32% state 5%”

The rule of 72 (72 ÷ rate) beside the exact doubling time, ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + rate). Dividend yield is a year's dividend over the price (quarterly and monthly payments multiplied out). Tax-equivalent yield is a tax-free yield ÷ (1 − your tax rate), with a state rate combined when the bond is free of it too. Not tax advice. ↵ copies the result.
