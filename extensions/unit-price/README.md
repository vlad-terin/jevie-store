# Unit Price

Which pack is the better buy, as a card while you type: the price per ounce, gram, litre or item, side by side.

## Type

- “unit price 4.99 for 12 oz vs 7.49 for 20 oz”
- “unit price 3.20 for 500 g vs 5.50 for 1 kg”
- “unit price 12 for 24 cans vs 8 for 12 cans”

It compares two packs or more by price per unit: per ounce (pounds become ounces), per 100 g (kilos become grams), per litre (millilitres, fluid ounces) or per item. Packs measured in different kinds of unit are not compared. ↵ copies the verdict.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
