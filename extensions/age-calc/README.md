# Age

An age from a birth date as a card while you type - in years, months and days, a baby's weeks, and the next birthday.

## Type

- “age born 1990-05-04”
- “baby age born mar 3 2026”
- “how old born 4 may 1990”

From a birth date (2026-03-03, 3/3/2026, Mar 3 2026 or 3 March 2026), it gives the age in years, months and days, the days lived, and the next birthday. Under two, it counts weeks too. ↵ copies the age.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
