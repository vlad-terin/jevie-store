# Business Math

Margin and markup, growth rate (CAGR) and break-even, as cards while you type - the numbers a price, a plan or a pitch needs.

## Type

- “margin cost 25 price 40”
- “price for 40% margin on 25”
- “markup 25 40”
- “cagr 100 to 250 in 5 years”
- “break even fixed 50k price 120 cost 45”

Margin is profit over price, markup is profit over cost; both are shown, since they are confused so often. Amounts take k, m and b. ↵ copies the line.
