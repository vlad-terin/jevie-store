# Business Days

Working days, as a card while you type: 10 business days from today, workdays until a date, or between two - weekends and (optionally) US federal holidays left out.

## Type

- “10 business days from today”
- “15 working days from mar 3”
- “workdays until dec 15”
- “business days between oct 1 and nov 15”

Weekends are always left out. US federal holidays are left out too (observed dates, as the courts and banks count them), unless “settings for business-days” says none. ↵ copies the date or the count.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
