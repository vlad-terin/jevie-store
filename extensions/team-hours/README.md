# Team Hours

When a team across cities can meet, as a card while you type: the hours everyone is in their working day, in your time and theirs.

## Type

- “overlap new york london”
- “team hours san francisco berlin tokyo”
- “meeting time sf nyc london 8-6”

It knows about 100 cities and their time zones, daylight saving included. The working day is 9 to 5 on weekdays unless named (“8-6”). It finds the longest stretch in the next 24 hours when every place is working, in your time and in theirs. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
