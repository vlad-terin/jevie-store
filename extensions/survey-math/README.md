# Survey Math

A survey's margin of error from its responses, and the responses needed for a margin, as cards while you type - with the population when it is small.

## Type

- “margin of error 400 responses”
- “margin of error 250 of 1200”
- “responses needed 5%”
- “responses needed 3% population 2000 99%”

Margin of error is z × √(0.25 ÷ n), at the widest case (a 50% answer), 95% confidence unless you say 90% or 99%, and corrected for a small population when you give it. Responses needed solves the same for n. It assumes a random sample: who chose to answer is a bias no margin covers. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
