# Clinical Scores

eGFR by CKD-EPI 2021 with the CKD stage, pack-years and the Glasgow Coma Scale as cards while you type - for checking arithmetic.

## Type

- “egfr creatinine 1.1 age 60 female”
- “pack years 20 a day 30 years”
- “gcs e4 v5 m6”

eGFR by the 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation (no race term) from creatinine in mg/dL, age and sex, with the KDIGO G stage. Pack-years are packs a day × years; a number of 5 or more without “packs” is read as cigarettes, 20 to a pack. The Glasgow Coma Scale adds eye, verbal and motor. For checking arithmetic, not diagnosis. ↵ copies the result.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
