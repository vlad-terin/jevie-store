# Freelance Rate

The hourly and day rate a freelancer needs to reach a yearly income, as a card while you type - with billable hours, overhead and tax counted.

## Type

- “freelance rate 120k”
- “freelance rate 150k 1000 billable hours 20% overhead 30% tax”

It grosses the take-home up for tax (30% unless named) and business costs (20%), then divides by billable hours: 1,200 a year unless named, since admin, sales, holidays and gaps take the rest of a working year. ↵ copies the rate.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
