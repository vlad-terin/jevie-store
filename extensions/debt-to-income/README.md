# Debt to Income

A debt-to-income ratio as a card while you type - monthly debts over gross monthly income - and where lenders draw their lines.

## Type

- “dti debts 2000 income 6500”
- “debt to income 1800 a month income 90k a year”

Debt to income is monthly debt payments (loans, credit cards' minimums, the housing payment) over gross monthly income; an income of 30,000 or more, or one marked “a year”, is divided by 12. It shows the room left under 36%, and where 43% and 50% sit. Lenders differ. ↵ copies the ratio.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
