# Savings Goal

How much to save a month to reach a goal by a date, as a card while you type - with interest, and what you have already.

## Type

- “savings goal 20000 in 3 years at 4%”
- “savings goal 50k in 5 years at 5% have 10k”
- “savings goal 8000 in 18 months”

The monthly deposit that, with what you have and interest compounded monthly (none unless you give a rate), reaches the goal by the end: deposits at each month's end. It shows how much is deposits and how much interest. ↵ copies the monthly amount.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
