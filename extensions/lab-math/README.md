# Lab Math

Bench sums as cards while you type: a dilution by C1V1 = C2V2, the mass for a molar solution, and a stock's molarity from a percentage.

## Type

- “dilution 10 m to 1 m 50 ml”
- “dilute 1 mg/ml to 50 ug/ml 2 ml”
- “molarity 0.5 m 250 ml mw 58.44”

Dilution is C1V1 = C2V2: the stock volume, the diluent to add, and the fold. Concentrations must be the same kind: molar (M, mM, µM, nM), mass (mg/mL, µg/mL, ng/mL), percent or X. The molar card gives the grams to weigh, to be made up to the volume. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
