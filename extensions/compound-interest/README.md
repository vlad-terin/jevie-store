# Compound Interest

What savings or an investment grows to, as a card while you type: a lump sum, monthly saving, or both - with how much is interest.

## Type

- “compound 10000 at 5% for 20 years”
- “save 500 a month at 7% for 10 years”
- “invest 10k plus 300 a month at 6% for 25 years”

It compounds monthly, with any monthly amount added at the end of each month. It shows what was put in, what it becomes, and how much of that is interest. Before tax and inflation; the Inflation card turns it into today's money. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

Nothing: it is worked out on your Mac. It changes nothing.
