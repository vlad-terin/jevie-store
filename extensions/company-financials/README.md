# Company Financials

A US-listed company's latest annual numbers, as a card while you type: revenue and its growth, net income and margin, EPS and cash - from its SEC filings (EDGAR).

## Type

- “financials aapl”
- “revenue msft”
- “earnings nvda”
- “financials apple”

It reads the company's XBRL facts from its 10-K filings: revenue and its growth over the year before, net income and margin, diluted EPS, operating income and cash. A ticker or a company's name finds it in SEC's list. SEC asks every program to identify itself, so the email you set in “settings for company-financials” goes in the request to sec.gov, and nowhere else. ↵ copies the line.

## Reaches

www.sec.gov and data.sec.gov (your contact email in the User-Agent, as SEC requires). It changes nothing.
